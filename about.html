ABOUT ME / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

WHO I AM

Hello! I’m Truong Thong.
You can call me Tom.

I’m Nguyen Thanh Truong Thong, a senior graphic designer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. My journey began with mathematics, moved into graphic design, and grew through agency teams, retail, e-commerce, and coffee brands.

I care about the idea behind an identity and the system that keeps it consistent. My strengths connect branding, concept development, visual systems, website and app design, and project management.

From Capcom and Nintendo to Neo Geo, games have been part of my life since childhood. Coffee, books, travel, and reading God’s Word give me space to pause, discover, and create with purpose.
