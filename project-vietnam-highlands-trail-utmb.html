01 / A SENSE OF PLACE.

A sense of place.

Landscape, trail running and the “Meet you in the Highlands” message bring the campaign together. The Pinhatt landscape and runner-led direction explore two ways into the same highland story.

02 / ONE SAOLA. MANY STORIES.

One saola. Many stories.

A flexible system of saola motifs, local icons and cultural patterns draws on the region’s mountains, forests, flowers and cultural references. Orange, deep green and warm neutrals connect the applications.

03 / SOMETHING WORTH KEEPING.

Something worth keeping.

Finisher medal concepts translate the saola, mountain landscape and floral motifs into a physical keepsake. Silver relief and orange accents give the different directions a shared visual identity.

04 / BEYOND THE FINISH LINE.

Beyond the finish line.

The identity extends into apparel and everyday objects: running jackets, T-shirts, headwear, socks, totes, drinkware and accessories. Patterns and the saola motif adapt across the collection while preserving a recognisable family.
