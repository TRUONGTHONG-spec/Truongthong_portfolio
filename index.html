A BIT MORE THAN PIXELS

Designer by craft.
Player at heart.

I’m Nguyen Thanh Truong Thong — also known as Tom Nguyen — a senior graphic designer based in Vietnam. My experience spans coffee, retail, e-commerce, and agency environments, with a focus on digital design, POSM, illustration, and visual storytelling.

I grew up with the worlds of Capcom, Nintendo, and Neo Geo. That sense of discovery still shapes the way I create: curious, expressive, and always ready to try another approach.

Away from the screen, you’ll find me with a cup of coffee, a good book, planning a trip, or spending time in God’s Word. Faith keeps me grounded. Curiosity keeps me moving.
